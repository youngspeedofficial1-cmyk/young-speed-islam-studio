# Lessons from top-performing reference videos (analysed 2026-11-23)

## A. "A billion good deeds in just thirty seconds" (animated, English, 57 s, -13.9 LUFS)
- Hook = one huge number that keeps ticking up on screen ("906,085,650 GOOD DEEDS") under a bold promise.
- Credibility line in the first 10 s: "These are not my words. These are the words of the Prophet ﷺ."
- Share hook early, tied to reward ("Share this and you earn ... even after you die") - only with a verified source
  (e.g. Muslim 1893, reward like the doer for whoever guides to good); never invent a reward.
- Participation: "Come on, say it with me" -> the dhikr is shown big in Arabic, translation small underneath,
  reference in small caps ("1 OF 3 · SAHIH MUSLIM 2726"), and three progress dots that fill one by one.
  The viewer does something during the video, which holds watch time.
- One visual metaphor that grows with every repetition (a mountain of gold coins under a night sky).
- Close: "Now imagine someone learned this because of you" -> brand end card.
- Look: deep purple night + gold, crescent moon, soft particles; bold rounded sans, gold keywords, text in the top third.

## B. "Sirf 3 Aayatein ... lekin paighaam bahut bada" (Surah al-Kawthar explainer, Roman Urdu, 67 s)
- Hook = small number + big promise ("Only 3 verses").
- Agenda card right after the hook: "AAJ KYA JAANENGE: 01 Kyun utri ye surah? 02 Teen paigham 03 Zindagi se rishta".
  A numbered roadmap opens three loops, so viewers stay to see them closed.
- Chapter / key-term title cards between parts: small kicker ("EK ZAROORI BAAT") + big word ("IKHTILAF", "ABTAR").
- Cinematic, photoreal stills (desert town, a doorway, footsteps) with slow push-ins and a warm amber grade,
  letterboxed 16:9 inside the vertical frame; photo-card ("polaroid") inserts; numbered verse boxes (1 2 3 4 ?).
- Captions: Roman Urdu, 3-5 words at a time, the current word highlighted in a gold box (karaoke style).
- Narration never stops: almost no silence in either video (0-2 % quiet frames). Holds of 2-3 s with nothing
  said lose viewers; keep pauses short and let the visuals carry them.

## What we take into our engine / scripts
1. Hooks: a concrete number or a "only X" promise; on-screen counters where the topic has a number.
2. "These are the words of the Prophet ﷺ" credibility line early when the video is a hadith.
3. Agenda card (01/02/03) after the hook for videos over 60 s; chapter title cards between acts.
4. Participation segments for dhikr/dua topics: "say it with me", 3 dots, "1 OF 3 · <ref>".
5. Smaller, cleaner citations in small caps under the Arabic.
6. Option for Roman-Urdu captions (many young Pakistani viewers read Roman Urdu faster than Nastaliq).
7. Keep holds short (<= 1.2 s except the final card); no dead air.
8. A brand end card (channel name + follow) as the last 2 s.
9. Photoreal scenes need images we do not generate here; if the user supplies images, use them with slow
   push-ins and a warm grade. Never use images of prophets, companions or faces of real people.
